Etherphone Guide

Etherphone is a gesture-controlled live instrument: your hands choose the chord, the voicing, and the expression. It is built on Gesture Synth by Eric Wei, and the gesture language below is his.

Instruments

The picker holds every instrument: the synths, a set of wavetables, and two sampled instruments. Load Wavetable… reads a single-cycle .wav and rebuilds it as a playable table; Load Sample… reads any recording and pitches it across the keyboard. Both add the file to the picker and switch to it.

Left Hand

Tilt
Inward → Major
Outward → Minor

Fingers (Scale Degree)
These follow the selected gesture map. The default, Classic, is the vocabulary inherited from Gesture Synth; Triad and Pentatonic are smaller sets. The on-screen guide always shows the active map.

Right Hand

Fingers (Chord Quality)
1 → Root Position
2 → 1st Inversion
3 → Major/Minor 7th
4 → Dominant/Diminished 7th

Octave
Thumb In → higher octave
Thumb Out → lower octave

Tilt
Inward → More Filter
Outward → Less Filter

Height
Higher → Louder
Lower → Softer
